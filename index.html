#the flywheel

Launches pay for the machines. The machines pay for the token. The token makes the next launch cheaper.

  1. →More validators launched. Each one is a monthly hosting and management customer.
  2. →More hosting revenue. Fees scale with the number of live nodes, not with trading volume.
  3. →Revenue funds $VALIDATOR buybacks. A share of platform revenue buys the token on the open market.
  4. →Better infrastructure and incentives. Holder discounts and better hardware pull in the next wave of operators.

what one click can't do

We remove the technical work. The economics of running a Solana validator stay the same.

hardware
Validators run on real, high-spec servers. Someone pays for them every month, and that someone is the operator.
vote fees
Validators pay SOL in vote transaction fees every epoch, whether or not they have stake.
stake
Without enough delegated stake, commission won't cover costs. Building a community that stakes is your job.
rewards
Earnings depend on network performance and stake, and aren't guaranteed. This isn't financial advice.
